Privacy Policy

Pondlewood Friends and the makerkids tool · Last updated October 22, 2026

Pondlewood Friends is a YouTube channel of wordless cartoons for young children. "makerkids" is a private tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer to make episodes and upload them to the channel. This page explains what data the tool uses.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner signs in to makerkids. It is not offered to the public, and it never asks viewers, children or anyone else to sign in.

What data it uses

Through YouTube API Services, the tool uploads videos to the owner's own channel, reads the channel name to make sure it uploads to the right channel, and reads that channel's view and revenue reports. It does not read data about viewers or any other Google account.

Where the data goes

The sign-in token and reports stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Viewers watch the videos on YouTube, which handles their data under its own policies. The channel is set as made for kids, so YouTube turns off comments and personalized ads on it.

YouTube and Google policies

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account permissions page.
